How it started

The rivalry began in 1965, per Weber School District. Why it began is not documented — there is no recorded school split or boundary fight behind it, just two Weber County schools a short drive apart that have played every year since.

The trophy

An elaborate shield carrying the seals of both schools. Roy's head coach has called it one of the best-looking trophies in the state. The year it was introduced is not documented.

The games that made it

August 30, 2024 produced the highest-scoring game in series history, Roy 48-34 over an unbeaten Weber, with Roy's Robert Young scoring five touchdowns including a 74-yard run to put it away. In 2020 Weber won on a blocked field goal and a goal-line stand to take a share of the Region 1 title. Weber took the 2026 meeting 53-35 behind six touchdowns from quarterback Carter Payne. A live subplot: Weber head coach Mo Cannon is a former Roy assistant, and is 2-1 in the game since taking the Weber job in 2024.

Traditions

Large crowds and, in the district's own words, an electric atmosphere. Little else is documented.

Where it stands

Both are Region 1 schools. Now played as an early-season Thursday night fixture.

On the record

A separately published figure has 34–25 (Weber School District, 2024-08-30). The two figures are not blended. Reported entering the 2024 game.

What we could not find

Why the rivalry started. The Shield's introduction year and who commissioned it. Longest streaks either way.
